# Battle Arena

A fast-paced arena shooter prototype developed for **GAM 207: Game Development**.

**Battle Arena** puts the player into a compact combat arena where they must fight against AI-controlled opponents, manage their health, and eliminate enemies as quickly and accurately as possible. The prototype focuses on responsive movement, mouse aiming, continuous shooting, and chaotic multi-enemy encounters.

## About the Game

Battle Arena is a competitive-style arena shooter centered around **skill, accuracy, reflexes, and situational awareness**.

Players enter an arena where AI-controlled enemies engage in combat. The player must navigate the environment, avoid incoming attacks, and eliminate opponents while managing multiple threats at once.

The goal is to achieve the highest possible score by defeating enemies before the round ends or the player's health is depleted.

## Features

* Fast-paced arena combat
* AI-controlled enemies
* WASD player movement
* Mouse-controlled aiming and camera movement
* Continuous shooting with the left mouse button
* Player health system
* Enemy health bars
* Score-based gameplay
* Compact combat arena
* Environmental obstacles and cover
* Multiple enemies attacking from different directions
* Minimal and easy-to-understand interface

## Gameplay

The player spawns directly into the arena and must immediately react to the surrounding enemies. AI opponents engage in combat while the player moves through the environment and looks for opportunities to eliminate targets.

Successful gameplay requires accurate shooting, movement, and awareness of enemies approaching from different directions. As combat continues, the player works toward achieving a high score while attempting to survive until the end of the round.

## Goal

The primary objective is to eliminate as many enemies as possible and achieve the highest score.

Players must balance offensive and defensive play by accurately targeting enemies while avoiding damage. Positioning, movement, and situational awareness are important for surviving encounters with multiple opponents.

## Arena Design

The game takes place in a compact arena designed around continuous movement and combat.

Obstacles and areas of cover are placed throughout the environment to give players opportunities to reposition and avoid incoming attacks. The layout encourages players to remain mobile rather than staying in one location, while allowing firefights to occur from multiple directions.

## Art Direction

Battle Arena uses a combination of realistic and stylized elements to maintain visual clarity during fast-paced combat.

Characters and enemies are designed with distinct silhouettes to make them easier to identify during gameplay. Health bars and other UI elements use clear visual indicators so players can quickly understand their current status and the condition of nearby enemies.

## Interface

The interface is intentionally minimal so that the player can focus on combat.

The player's health bar is displayed in the lower-left portion of the screen, while enemy health bars appear above individual enemies. This provides important information without obstructing the player's view of the arena.
